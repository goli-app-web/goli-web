Delete Account

Last updated · September 2026

You can permanently delete your Goli account and all associated data directly from within the app. No web login is required — account deletion is a self-service feature built into the app.

01 Steps to delete your account

  1. Open the Goli app and make sure you're logged in
  2. Go to the Settings tab
  3. Tap your profile card at the top to open Edit Profile
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account
  5. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete account

02 What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — email, username, display name, profile photo
  • All your match predictions (tips) across every group
  • Your season predictions and points history
  • Your membership in any groups (group data owned by other members is not affected)
  • Your push notification token
Retention — your data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Push notification tokens are removed immediately.

03 Can't access the app?

If you're unable to log in and need your account and data deleted, email us at info@goli-app.com from the email address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion manually.
